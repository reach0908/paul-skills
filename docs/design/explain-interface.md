## What it does

Explain how a specific site or visual effect works, using the evidence available from its rendered page or source. Measurements, calculations and inferences remain distinct.

## When to reach for it

Type `/explain-interface`, or the agent reaches for it automatically when you ask how someone else's site or effect was built. Reviewing your own interface stays with `better-interface`.

Use it when a reference interests you and you want to understand its mechanism. It explains the named subject rather than judging its quality or rebuilding it.

## Common questions

**Can a screenshot prove how the original was implemented?**

No. An image supports a plausible reconstruction, not source claims. The explanation labels scale assumptions and estimates; a live page may supply computed styles and animation evidence that the image cannot.

## It's working if

- The explanation stays on the named effect or site scope.
- Layers and their perceptual roles explain the mechanism instead of dumping values.
- Unknowns, estimates and non-transferable assets are clearly identified.
