# Moderator synthesis

Read the raw attempts before assigning outcomes. Record target revision or content digest, URL, seed state, actual host/browser/session/device, tasks, bounds, independence and any intervention. Missing exact model/version/cost telemetry is unknown, not zero. Keep failed and unavailable attempts visible.

Preserve the original participant packet and its boundary from the author packet. Verify that a fresh-context launch occurred. If the same context saw author intent, prior findings or another participant's output, label that exposure and narrow its claim. Preserve evidence with private state in the owning project; public reports use permitted synthetic or redacted evidence.

## Intent comparison

| Intent and source | Intended outcome | Actual action/result and evidence | Alignment | Next decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Alignment is aligned, mismatch, not reached, or intent unknown. Test an outcome rather than a prescribed route unless the route is itself an explicit requirement. Distinguish selected, saved, shared and completed through visible outcomes and the author's contract; a click or toast alone may not establish persistence or delivery.

Then summarize:

- What these participants understood and achieved without guidance.
- Where visible behavior diverged from intent, including alternative successful paths.
- What the post-task interview adds, separately from observed behavior.
- Which questions were never reached and which conditions were simulated.
- The smallest consequential change or follow-up, with an owner.

Group a repeated symptom without multiplying it by screenshot count. Prioritize by effect on the stated task and strength of evidence, rather than invented numerical risk scores. A participant's expectation is evidence of that AI interpretation, not automatic authority to change product policy.

Product ambiguity returns to a decision. A navigation/recovery issue goes to user-flow; a layout, label, focus or other interface concern goes to its design owner. The moderator does not award a product approval score. A human study can use these observations to improve its task guide, but must obtain its own participant evidence.

The output is a traceable walkthrough report, not a new PRD. If updating the canonical brief is requested, pass only accepted product decisions and separately identified proposals, preserving unaffected choices.
