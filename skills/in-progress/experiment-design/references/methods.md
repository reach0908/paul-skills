# Match evidence to the decision

Read the row for the selected question. These are method options, not mandatory experiments or evidence that a study has run.

| Question | Useful small test | What it can and cannot show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demand | Observe a real workaround or offer a manual/concierge version with a meaningful voluntary commitment | Interest, actual use, repeat use and payment are distinct signals. Define the exact demand claim. A signup alone cannot establish repeated value or willingness to pay. |
| Usability | Give intended users a task without teaching the answer; observe completion, confusion and recovery | Specify the starting context and what counts as completing the product task. Record any guidance. A prototype can test comprehension, but simulated saving cannot prove persistence. UI author walkthroughs and accessibility QA supply separate evidence. |
| AI prototype walkthrough | Let fresh agents attempt neutral tasks, then interview them and compare recorded behavior with author intent | Exploratory AI task evidence, useful for finding misunderstandings and improving a later study. A coached pass is assisted; AI attempts are not human participants or demand evidence. |
| AI quality | Compare outputs on the same representative inputs with a reference/baseline and a task-relevant assessment | Identify who judges, whether order is blinded, failure types and repeat variability. An author can do a bounded exploratory rating, labelled as such. Offline ratings cannot establish live user benefit, retention or demand. |
| Technical feasibility | Run a bounded spike or existing test on representative conditions | Capture artifact/version, workload, limits and failures. A local or mock pass applies only to those conditions. Broader runtime, deployment and product-effect claims need their own observations. |

Use only the observation needed for the current decision. If a test's required sample or threshold lacks a basis, explain the proposed basis or leave it unknown; do not manufacture certainty. Record remaining validity limits and what would resolve them.
