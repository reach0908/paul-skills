## Criteria fixed before outputs

- break: import the real component, leave production source/style unchanged, infer applicable axes, preselect plausible long content and narrow containers, make one observation pass, report visible failures accurately, annotate the page, and retain it. Compare actual long/narrow coverage and rendered overflow. A supported wrapping case must not be falsely reported as overflowing. Counts are denominators, not quality scores.
- user-flow: initiating is distinct from confirmed success; uncertain outcome cannot be resent; status checks and receipt preserve identity; closing/reopening covers every state where the table offers closing, including before start and after confirmation; retained key/receipt depends on state. Unresolved auth and permission rules stay unresolved. No UI composition or implementation.
- Eight explicit invocation probes: `$name` alone must load the intended local skill body and describe its bounded role without executing the simulated task. Recognize host-injected bodies as well as tool reads. Merely mentioning a skill is not a load.
- Four neighboring natural requests must not load explicit-only state-machine, break, explain-interface or variant. Positive implicit selection is tested in the chain below, not inferred from catalog availability. Claude invocation policy is checked statically only.
- Four fresh natural-language stages: IA, flow, wireframe, build. Do not name the target skill in the prompt. Each consumes the actual predecessor's artifact, with a recorded hash. Preserve stable destination/task/transition identifiers, open policy decisions, unknown-outcome semantics, draft and return context. Wireframe is exercised at 1280x900 and 320x700; build receives a local fixture-approved styling specification and must preserve interactions. Record missing automatic selection separately from output quality.

## Execution and limits

Initial budget: 24 contexts (8 comparison, 8 explicit, 4 negative, 4 chain). Maximum four targeted retests after a recorded failure. Soft target five minutes per implementation; hard limit fifteen minutes, with timeout recorded as incomplete, never silently discarded. Runs may execute concurrently when inputs are independent; chain stages are sequential. Every input, command trace, final output, elapsed time and available browser evidence is retained. No global install, source revision advancement, publication or promotion.

One deterministic fixture per category cannot establish universal superiority, real-user usability or model efficiency. Promotion assessment must distinguish source-relative evidence, invocation evidence, handoff evidence and user acceptance. Six other role comparisons from the preceding report remain valid only for their recorded fixture and unchanged skill hashes; this trial adds invocation/handoff evidence rather than silently rerunning all their quality tasks.
