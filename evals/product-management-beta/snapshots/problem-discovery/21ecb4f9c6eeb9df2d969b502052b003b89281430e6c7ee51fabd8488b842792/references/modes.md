# Question posture

Read this only when the supplied context leaves a consequential problem question unanswered.

| Purpose | Useful questions | Evidence to seek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business validation | What specific situation triggers the need? What workaround does that person use? What behavior shows the problem matters? What is the smallest useful first offer? | Concrete behavior and its source; separate interest, commitment, repeated use and payment. Use anonymous descriptions where sufficient. |
| Personal or internal tool | Which recurring task or experience should change? What is awkward today? What would make the first version useful to its actual user? | The author's or team's concrete workflow and constraints. Commercial proof is optional when it is outside the goal. |
| Learning or playful exploration | What experience is worth trying? What nearby tool is closest? What is the fastest usable or shareable slice? | A scene, learning question or desired experience. Offer an adjacent possibility only when exploration is wanted, labelled as an AI suggestion. |

Use these as a question pool, not a questionnaire. Match specificity to the next decision. Challenge an unsupported claim by naming the missing evidence and what would change the conclusion. Avoid treating absence of a workaround as proof that the opportunity is worthless. An explicit solution still needs only the missing problem context, not a restart of the idea.
