# Existing-plan failure review

Read only when assessing how an existing product or launch plan might fail.

Imagine the plan reaching its intended outcome unsuccessfully. Work backward to a plausible cause from product value, usability and implementation perspectives. Separate an evidenced threat, a plausible but unsupported concern and an unspoken assumption. Tie each to a plan decision and its evidence, rather than declaring every unknown a launch blocker.

Pick the assumption whose failure changes the next commitment and return to the test-design step. Keep plan dates and resource limits supplied by the user; propose missing bounds explicitly. Avoid importing a default 14-day launch, 30-day follow-up or a named person's ownership. The review ends with a testable question and decision, not a second strategy document.
