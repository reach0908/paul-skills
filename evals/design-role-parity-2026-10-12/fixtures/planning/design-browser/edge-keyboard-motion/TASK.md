# Observe responsive, keyboard and motion behavior
Serve this local app with `python3 serve.py` and use the installed Playwright CLI. At 1280x900 and 390x844, inspect collapsed and expanded scan details. Using actual keyboard actions, expand details, start a refresh, cancel it before completion, then refresh to completion. Repeat refresh with reduced motion enabled and report the observed motion and final state. Save the action log, structured measurements and relevant captures in `evidence/`. Record the actual browser, viewport and reduced-motion setting; do not infer interaction or motion from a static image. Do not fix the app or measure automation duration as product performance. Close only your own browser session and server.
