# Community kitchen PM brief
Revision: CK-PM-7. Accepted scope, 2026-09-22.
Audience: neighborhood residents who reserve a shared kitchen for small food projects.
Retrieval tasks:
- T1: Find a kitchen with an oven that fits four people next Saturday.
- T2: Check whether Kitchen B has a mixer, then return to the filtered list.
- T3: Find the check-in instructions for my confirmed booking.
Fixed decisions: signed-out people can browse kitchens, equipment and availability. Personal bookings require sign-in. Each kitchen has one stable detail destination. Availability is a date filter, not a separate product. Equipment is an attribute of a kitchen; equipment rental is out of scope. An account settings destination already exists at /account.
Content: kitchen records, equipment descriptions, time slots, confirmed personal bookings, check-in instructions and general access rules. Booking check-in instructions are only for the relevant booking owner. General access rules are public. No maps, prices, purchases, social profiles or organizer tools in this slice.
A narrow viewport must retain access to discovery, personal bookings and the selected kitchen's details. No navigation component has been selected. This brief is accepted; the future IA is not yet user-approved.
